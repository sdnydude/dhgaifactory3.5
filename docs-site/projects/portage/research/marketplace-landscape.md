## What this means for Portage

The large used-goods audiences in both regions sit behind marketplaces with no open seller API. Facebook Marketplace (allow-list via a Facebook representative), OfferUp, Mercari (US and Japan), Karrot, Bunjang, Carousell and Gumtree all lack an API Portage can register for. Where open or near-open seller APIs exist, they mostly serve business sellers of new goods: Amazon (Professional plan only), Rakuten, Coupang, Naver, Shopee, Lazada, Flipkart.

The small set of open APIs that fit personal effects is eBay and Reverb (already live), Discogs, HipStamp, Delcampe, Mercado Libre Mexico, and Etsy. Etsy's new Seller App tier approves own-shop apps within minutes, but a multi-user Portage still needs commercial access. Nextdoor has a closed-beta partner endpoint that creates and marks sold For Sale & Free posts.

Three findings change current plans and need an operator decision. They are open questions 1–3 at the end.

1. **OfferUp's terms cover the assisted-post extension.** The ToS (last updated July 21, 2026) separately prohibits "Develop or use any third-party applications that interact with the OfferUp Services without OfferUp's prior written consent" ([offerup.com/terms](https://offerup.com/terms), fetched 2026-10-04). That clause is independent of whether a human presses Post.
2. **Japan's C2C market is closed to US sellers.** Mercari JP (ToS 2.1.17: users are 日本在住の個人, individuals resident in Japan), Yahoo!オークション (guideline: 日本国内に居住していること, residing in Japan) and Rakuma (rule prohibiting 日本国内に居住しない者による登録および利用, registration and use by non-residents) all restrict sellers to residents. All three also ban unapproved bots or tools. Japan is a market only for Japan-resident Portage users.
3. **Browser-extension posting carries ToS exposure almost everywhere.** Most Shape B candidates have a clause against automated means. Several name third-party tools, software or browser add-ons explicitly: OfferUp, Nextdoor ("browser plugins, and add-ons"), Vinted ("external software tools"), Yahoo! Auctions and Yahoo!フリマ (auto-listing tools), Rakuma (BOT やツール), and Bunjang (자동 접속 프로그램, with 3/15/30-day escalating bans). Gumtree AU, Carousell, Shopee and Trade Me ban bots, scrapers and automated means without naming third-party tools.

## Methodology

- **Classification.** Shape A = public, documented seller API with listing create and update/end, order read, and OAuth or token auth. Read-only, affiliate and catalogue-feed APIs were classified Shape B. Shape B = no usable seller API, plus a logged-in web post form a browser extension could fill.
- **Extension reuse.** Judged against the Portage Assistant as built. The background worker, page bridge, guarded photo fetch and review panel are site-agnostic. The fill engine (`apps/extension/src/fill-engine.ts`) is OfferUp-shaped: one modal, React-controlled inputs, condition chips, a category dialog, a ZIP dialog, and a `DataTransfer` file input.
  - "new form map": a similar single form, where a new `form-map.json` and a small engine generalisation would work.
  - "new content script": wizards, iframes, drag-only photo upload, captchas, or a very different structure.
  - "not feasible": app-only posting, or consignment and buy-out services with nothing to post.
- **Evidence.** Every cell carries a URL fetched 2026-10-04 and a verbatim snippet in the source data. Japan and Korea were searched and quoted in Japanese and Korean, with translations. Missing figures are "not published"; estimates are labelled.
  - Confidence: High = primary source fetched and current. Medium = secondary source, or a primary older than 12 months. Low = single weak source.
- **Agents.**
  - First pass: 6 Sonnet research agents (NA general, NA verticals, Japan, Korea, APAC-rest, competitors).
  - Gap-fills: 3 Sonnet agents (NA general, NA verticals, APAC-rest) retried pages that blocked automated fetches, using help-center JSON endpoints, SEC filings and direct fetches.
  - Review: 2 independent Sonnet reviewers re-fetched every Japan and Korea cell and returned CONFIRMED, CORRECTED or UNVERIFIABLE per cell (38 corrections).
  - Main session (Opus) checks: re-downloaded the cited page for 26 key claims and string-matched the quoted text; 23 matched outright. The other 3 were confirmed separately: Karrot's MAU sentence was located in its press release, and Shopee's 6-K figures and Facebook's allow-list sentence were confirmed by WebFetch. Three findings from the last agent (OfferUp's third-party-application clause, Etsy's Seller App, and the eBay deprecation dates) were also confirmed by direct fetch.
  - This check caught one fabrication: the fetch tool's summariser had invented Korean filing thresholds on a gov.kr page. The Korea reviewer replaced them with statute text from law.go.kr.
- **Scoring.** `score = 0.35·S + 0.25·T + 0.20·F + 0.10·C + 0.10·R`, each input 0–5. The inputs appear in every table row as S, T, F, C and R.
  - **S, sellers:** log10, min-max scaled within the region. Every region has fewer than three published seller counts with a comparable definition, so S is a neutral 2.5 everywhere and does not separate rows. This is a data gap, not a judgment.
  - **T, buyer traffic or users:** log10, min-max scaled within the region. Definitions differ (MAU, yearly active users, monthly visits, active buyers) and are shown per row. Treat T as an order-of-magnitude signal. Missing T uses the regional median ("median: T").
  - **F, fit with personal-effects and used goods:** 5 general used-goods C2C · 4 used vertical matching Portage inventory · 3 mixed new/used · 2 authentication or consignment model · 1 new-goods B2C · 0 incompatible.
  - **C, integration cost:** 5 Shape A open · 4 Shape A gated · 3 Shape B new form map · 2 Shape B new content script or unverified reuse · 1 Shape A partner-only or deprecated · 0 not feasible. Etsy is scored 4 (commercial access gated for multi-user). Trade Me is scored 3 (API closed to personal sellers).
  - **R, ToS risk (inverse):** 5 no clause found on a full read · 3 generic anti-bot or scraping clause · 1 clause naming third-party tools, software, programs or add-ons · 0 the same plus cited enforcement (Yahoo! Auctions, Rakuma, Bunjang, Poshmark). ToS not verified uses the regional median ("median: R").
  - Because S and T are scaled within each region, a score compares rows inside a region. The cross-region top 20 is a reading aid, not a strict ranking.

## North America

Facebook Marketplace and OfferUp lead on audience; neither has an open listing API. Meta's Commerce Platform distributes to Marketplace only for sellers a Facebook representative adds to an allow list ([source](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/commerce-platform/platforms/distribution.md)). Meta launched a first-party Seller app on 2026-07-24 with bulk listing and no third-party API ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/introducing-seller-app-facebook-marketplace/)).

Craigslist's bulk interface is limited to paid categories case by case, and its terms exclude software other than its app and general-purpose browsers.

Mercari US reported 4.2M Q4 MAU and US$810M FY2026.6 GMV (Mercari IR). Its sell page returns a bot-verification interstitial to automated browsers, so the reuse call is "new content script".

Depop's sale to eBay closed on 2026-07-30 (Etsy press release). Depop's Selling API is partner-only.

Among verticals, open or gated APIs exist for Discogs (open), HipStamp, Delcampe, Chrono24 and Back Market. Whatnot's Seller API is "Not accepting new applicants". TCGplayer is "no longer granting new API access". Most premium verticals (The RealReal, ThredUp, KEH, MPB, The Pro's Closet, Kaiyo, 1stDibs, Fanatics Collect, COMC) are consignment, buy-out or vetted-dealer models with nothing for a listing integration to post to.

eBay's deprecation page lists UploadSiteHostedPictures for decommission on 2026-09-30 and GeteBayDetails for 2027-03-15. Portage's publish path calls neither. GeteBayDetails appears only in the manual ops script `apps/api/src/scripts/ebay-shipping-services-probe.ts`. AddFixedPriceItem, ReviseFixedPriceItem, EndFixedPriceItem and GetItem are on neither list.

## Japan — deep dive

Japan's used-goods market is concentrated in three C2C services, and all three are closed to non-residents and hostile to tools.

- **Mercari (メルカリ).** MAU above 24M (2,419万) per FY2026.6 results; GMV of 1兆2,856億円 is from trade press, because the 決算短信 text was not reachable. Web posting exists: 「Webサイトから出品する場合は、商品の写真をご用意いただく必要があります。」 ("when listing from the website, you need to prepare item photos"), help article 821. Identity verification (本人確認) is app-only and requires a residence card. The prohibited-acts guide (article 900) bans 「弊社が提供するインターフェイスとは別の手法を用いてサービスにアクセスすること」 (accessing the service by a method other than the interface Mercari provides). Separately, ToS 4.3 bars business operators unless Mercari designates them.
- **Mercari Shops.** The only Mercari seller API: GraphQL with a sandbox, partner contract only, pre-registered IPs, and 10,000 points per hour. Used goods on Shops require a 古物商許可 (secondhand dealer licence).
- **Yahoo!オークション.** The Auction Web API ended between 2018 and 2020. Seller prohibited act 26 bans auto-listing tools (guideline revised 2026-07-13), and the eligibility article requires residence in Japan.
- **Yahoo!フリマ.** Web-browser listing launched 2025-12-03, and a PayPay account is no longer required. The guideline (revised 2026-08-27) bans auto-listing tools. No residency clause was found, so its eligibility is unverified rather than open.
- **Rakuma (楽天ラクマ).** The rules ban 「当社が許可していない BOT やツールなどの手段」 (BOTs, tools or similar means the company has not permitted) and prohibit registration by non-residents.
- **Jimoty (ジモティー).** Classifieds with web posting. Its terms' crawling clause concerns collecting posts, not posting.
- **Business channels with APIs.** Amazon.co.jp SP-API (Professional plan ¥4,900/month; "Individual accounts are not eligible"). Rakuten RMS (docs behind login; business only per secondary guides). Yahoo!ショッピング (store account; editItem limited to 1 query/sec). BASE (OAuth API with item add/edit/delete and orders, 5,000 requests/hour, API terms revised 2026-09-29).
- **Closed to individuals or buy-out only.** Digimart (「個人でのデジマートへの出店／出品は受け付けておりません。」, "individual stores or listings are not accepted") and Hard Off Netmall (store stock, 買取 buy-out).

**Regulatory flags for a reselling business.** These are for operator review and are not legal advice; the quotes and paraphrases come from the cited pages.

- Tokyo Metropolitan Police: a 古物商許可 is applied for at the police station for the principal place of business, and online sellers submit proof of the right to use their URL ([source](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/tetsuzuki/kobutsu/tetsuzuki/kyoka.html); fetch summary, not verbatim).
- Mercari's own statement: as a person-to-person venue, 古物営業法 "in principle does not apply" to individuals selling unwanted goods ([help 1856](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/1856/)). This is a platform statement, not a government one.
- Consumer Affairs Agency: a guideline on who counts as a 販売業者 (sales business) in internet auctions exists, but its PDF could not be read, so no criteria are quoted.
- National Tax Agency: issuing qualified invoices requires prior registration as an invoice issuer ([source](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/zeimokubetsu/shohi/keigenzeiritsu/invoice_about.htm)).

**Crosslisting tools in Japan** (eコンビニ, Torima, Fuma tools, Next Engine, CROSS MALL) target domestic sellers. None supports OfferUp or any US marketplace.

## South Korea — deep dive

- **Karrot (당근).** Company-stated MAU above 21M ("월간 활성 이용자 수(MAU) 2,100만 명 이상", 2025 results PR). WISEAPP's August 2026 app MAU is 23.17M, reported by DataNews on 2026-09-10. Karrot domains redirected US browsers to nextdoor.com during this run, so its Korean ToS and help center could not be read. Web posting of used goods is therefore unverified, and the "not feasible" reuse call is provisional.
- **Bunjang (번개장터).** Company-stated web plus app MAU of about 12M; WISEAPP app MAU 5.16M. Its 운영정책 (operations policy, effective 2026-08-05) bans automatic access programs with escalating 3/15/30-day suspensions. ToS 제19조 requires 통신판매업자 (registered mail-order businesses) and other profit sellers to join 프로상점 (Pro Shop).
- **Joonggonara (중고나라).** Individuals can transact only in the app since 2025-09-22 (official help center). The web 셀러지원센터 (Seller Center) needs business registration, closed to new sellers on 2026-07-08, and is being wound down after September 2026. Seller ToS 제14조 bans agents, robots, scripts and spiders.
- **API channels (all business-verification gated).** Coupang (WING keys, HMAC, 5 requests/second per vendor; 24.6M Q4 2025 product-commerce active customers per the 10-K). Naver Smart Store Commerce API (POST /v2/products, orders, OAuth2 client credentials, no published numeric limits). 11st Open API. Gmarket/Auction ESM.
- **Consignment, authentication or unverified.** Musinsa Used is consignment. KREAM is bid-and-inspect on catalogue SKUs. Hellomarket and FruitsFamily web posting are unverified.

**Regulatory flags.** Not legal advice; these come from statute text via the 국가법령정보센터 (National Law Information Center) Open API.

- 전자상거래법 (E-Commerce Act) 제12조 requires mail-order sellers to file with the FTC or the local authority.
- The FTC notice exempts sellers with fewer than 50 transactions in the prior year, or 간이과세자 (simplified VAT taxpayers). An earlier claim of a 4,800만원 (₩48M) volume test is not in the current notice.
- 시행령 (Enforcement Decree) 제13조 sends operators whose main office is abroad to file with the FTC.
- Platform policies: Joonggonara and Bunjang both require continuous sellers to register as businesses.

**Cross-border.** No Korean marketplace was confirmed open to a US-resident individual. Every service checked requires Korean identity verification (본인인증, typically via a Korean mobile carrier) or business registration, and none publishes a foreign-seller path.

**Crosslisting tools in Korea** (Sabangnet, PlayAuto) are B2B multichannel order and listing managers for registered businesses; no consumer C2C crosslister was found.

## Contradictions with existing Portage docs and KB

1. **`website/docs/competitive-analysis.md`, Strategic Priority 1: "Add Poshmark + Mercari adapters".** Neither has an open seller API. Poshmark has none, and its ToS bans automated collection. Mercari US access is partner-only, and its sell page bot-walls automated browsers. Both can only be Shape B extension targets with ToS exposure, not adapters like eBay and Reverb.
2. **The same page's competitor matrix and pricing (verified May 2026) are stale.**
   - Vendoo: $14.99–59.99, no free tier on the pricing page.
   - Crosslist: $29.99–44.99 plus a $4.99 AI add-on.
   - Flyp: 100-day free trial, then $9/month; 7 marketplaces including Vinted.
   - Underpriced AI: 8+ marketplaces, pay-as-you-go credits.
   - Nifty: now has AI listing generation.
   - Closo: $0–499/year.
   - 3Dsellers: 10 marketplaces.
   - Voolist: $19.99–49.99.
   - List Perfectly: AI listing generator.
   - PrimeLister: no price shown.
   - Crosslist Magic: rebranded as Listelf.
3. **The same page's "Re-enable Etsy — adapter parked pending API key approval".** Etsy's documentation now offers a Seller App path, approved "within minutes, with no manual review queue" but limited to the owner's own shop. Etsy has also said it is "revisiting our App Key application process". The blocker has changed shape: own-shop use looks unblocked, while multi-user use still needs commercial access.
4. **KB insight "OfferUp has NO web posting for consumer accounts" (2026-10-02).** This was already corrected by the operator and by Phase 0, since logged-in desktop posting exists. The insight is still in the KB and will mislead searches. This run's brief warned the agents about it, and no agent repeated it.
5. **CLAUDE.md, OfferUp section ("no public API … assisted post").** This is consistent with this run. It does not mention the ToS third-party-application clause, which is new information (finding 1 above).

## Open questions

1. **OfferUp ToS.** The terms prohibit third-party applications that interact with OfferUp without prior written consent. Should Portage ask OfferUp for written consent, add this clause to the in-extension consent screen, or keep the extension beta-only while deciding? (Recommendation: disclose it in the consent screen now and request consent before any wider beta.)
2. **Japan and Korea strategy.** The major C2C platforms are residents-only (Japan confirmed; Korea effectively, through identity verification). Should Japan and Korea work target Japan- and Korea-resident Portage users, which means localisation and a local entity, or cross-border sell-into-Asia channels (eBay, Mercari Global, Buyee)? The latter were not researched in this run.
3. **Etsy.** Should Portage use the Etsy Seller App path for the operator's own shop now, or wait for commercial access for multi-user?
4. **Nextdoor partner API.** Should Portage apply? It is closed beta and USD only, and the Member Agreement bans browser plugins, so the API is the only route.
5. **Karrot (KR and US).** Its Korean ToS, web posting, and the observed US redirect to nextdoor.com need a check from a Korean IP or a logged-in operator session.
6. **Logged-in confirmation of web post forms.** Bunjang, Hellomarket, FruitsFamily, SidelineSwap, Poshmark and Mercari US need it before any reuse call is treated as more than Medium.
7. **Seller counts.** Almost none are published, so S is neutral everywhere. Should run 2 add paid secondary data (Similarweb, data.ai) as labelled estimates?
8. **eBay.** UploadSiteHostedPictures passed its listed decommission date (2026-09-30) and GeteBayDetails decommissions 2027-03-15. Portage's publish path uses neither, but the ops probe script uses GeteBayDetails. Migrate it to the Metadata API before March 2027?
9. **Scope.** Europe, Latin America beyond Mexico, and the Middle East are run 2. When?
