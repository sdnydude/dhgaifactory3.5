## 1. Verdict (revised)

**Metrics and logs collection is healthy. The alert loop is broken end to end. Traces are dead. LLM observability exists only for Portage. Backups do not exist.**

- Prometheus: 17 active targets, all UP; 18 rules; 14 job labels. [R1][R2][R3] settle v1's disagreement.
- Logs: Alloy to Loki healthy. 26 of 42 dhg-* containers emitted in 24h; 16 silent (app-side, Alloy has no filter). v1 had this inverted. [R1][R2]
- **Alert loop broken.** The only receiver is the registry webhook. 117 incidents active, none ever acknowledged, a critical PostgresFatalError open since 2026-08-25, `avg_ttd/ttm/ttr` all null. No email, Slack, or push. [R4] The live consumer of incidents is `dhg-remediator`, which is unmonitored, polls page 1 only, and re-processes the same 50 open incidents every 30s, writing ~74,000 action rows per day (one incident has 13,472); `dhg_registry` grew 1231 MB to 1654 MB in 30 days. [R2][R4] Alertmanager itself is not a Prometheus target; `alertmanager_notifications_failed_total`, `prometheus_rule_evaluation_failures_total`, `prometheus_notifications_dropped_total` are unalerted. [R3]
- Tempo: zero spans in 19.5 days of process uptime (container created 2026-04-06; "ever" is inferred). medkb, the only wired producer, has had 0 `/v1/query` calls in 7 days, so cause = never invoked. [A1][R1][R2] Tempo is a cleanup item, not CRITICAL. [R3]
- Langfuse: healthy on dh40801. **Wired for Portage only**: `portage-api` on g700data1 carries `LANGFUSE_BASE_URL/PUBLIC_KEY/SECRET_KEY` and 252 traces have landed (newest 2026-09-02). Nothing in the AI Factory stack (registry, medkb, vs-engine, memreg) is wired. v1 said "wired to nothing"; wrong. [R1][R4]
- LangSmith: 24 `@traceable` import files (18 in `dhg-agents-cloud/src`). **The two in the live registry already fail**: `langsmith` is not installed in the registry container, so `registry/notification_service.py` and `registry/timeout_handler.py` raise ModuleNotFoundError on import. CME SLA timeout, escalation and HOLD notifications are dead in production today. [R2]
- Pydantic AI: one prototype file, not served. [A1][A5]
- **Backups: none.** One manual `dhg_registry` dump from 2026-07-07 inside the git worktree; nothing scheduled on g700data1 or dh40801; Langfuse Postgres, ClickHouse and MinIO (Portage's live traces) unbacked. This was acceptance criterion #53 of the Langfuse ship marked complete on 2026-07-29, recorded verbatim as undelivered; ACs 50-52 (dh40801 node-exporter, Langfuse probe, Langfuse pg_dump) were also cut. [R2][R4]

## 7. Target trace architecture [R3]

Judged options: (a) OTel to Langfuse OTLP only, retire Tempo; (b) OTel Collector fan-out to Tempo and Langfuse; (c) Tempo for infra spans, Langfuse for LLM spans. R3 defends (a): (b) adds a fourth daemon for a copy nobody queries; (c) presumes infra spans that never existed. Langfuse v3.224.1 accepts OTLP at `/api/public/otel/v1/traces` (verified: GET 405, POST 401 auth-gated; supported since v3.22.0). Retiring Tempo must also remove the Tempo datasource, Loki `derivedFields`, Prometheus exemplar destination, and the metrics_generator remote_write. **This is a proposal for Stephen's decision in Phase 3, not a made decision.**
